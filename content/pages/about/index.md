I'm a software engineer with 13+ years in web and product development: seven on web-to-print e-commerce and design tools, then 6+ at Mattermost on the realtime messaging and workflow tools teams rely on for mission-critical work.

I'm full-stack by range and UX by instinct. The details are where I live: thoughtful, interactive interfaces that feel natural, fluid drag-and-drop, and accessibility that isn't an afterthought. I own products end to end, from scoping and design through testing, CI/CD, and upkeep. I lead by example: setting technical direction, mentoring and learning from the engineers around me, and collaborating across teams to bring the work to completion.

Most recently, I led the frontend for Mattermost Docs, a sovereign wiki for air-gapped networks. Before that, I worked across the core platform, from flagship features such as Collapsed Reply Threads and Channel Bookmarks to UI infrastructure like the CSS Grid app shell and plugin APIs that made in-call chat threads in Mattermost Calls possible. I also built and supported major parts of Mattermost Playbooks, which turns incident response and other high-stakes procedures into repeatable, trackable runs.

I taught myself to code before modern AI tools existed. AI now multiplies that curiosity my effectiveness, but what matters most hasn't changed: problems solved, not code written.

I do my best work on teams that care about the user as much as the craft.

Day to day, I work in TypeScript, React, and Go, with PostgreSQL, WebSockets, Playwright, and GitHub Actions.
