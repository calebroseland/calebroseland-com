_Last updated September 28, 2026._

This is a personal website. It doesn't sell anything, show ads, or build profiles of the people who read it. This page explains the little it does handle.

## What the site doesn't do

- **No cookies.** Reading the site sets none.
- **No analytics or tracking.** There is no analytics service, advertising tag, tracking pixel, or social-media widget on any page.
- **No accounts for readers.** There is nothing to sign up for.

## What happens when you visit

**Hosting.** The site is served by [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Like any web host, Cloudflare handles the technical details of each request, such as your IP address, the page asked for, and your browser's user agent, to deliver the page and protect the site from abuse. It keeps basic request logs for a limited time. A backup copy of the site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), which handles requests to that copy in the same way.

**Fonts.** Text is set in fonts served by [Adobe Fonts](https://www.adobe.com/privacy/policies/adobe-fonts.html). Your browser downloads them from Adobe's servers, so Adobe receives the same technical request details. Adobe says it uses them to serve fonts and count usage for licensing, and that Adobe Fonts sets no cookies.

**Error reports.** When something breaks in your browser, the site may send a short error report so it can be fixed. Only about one error in ten is sent. A report contains the error message, the page's address, and your browser's user agent, and nothing that identifies you. Reports are kept in the host's logs for a limited time, then deleted.

## What stays on your device

If you choose a theme or create a custom one, that choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site looks the same next time. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

## Editing

The site's owner can sign in to edit it. Signing in stores a session in that browser and talks to [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), where the content lives. None of this applies to readers.

## Links to other sites

Pages link to other sites, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, and articles elsewhere. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy doesn't cover.

## Changes and contact

If what the site handles changes, this page will be updated, along with the date at the top. Questions are welcome through the details on the [contact side of the card](/contact).
